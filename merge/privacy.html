Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including: account information (username, email, password), profile information, project data, uploaded files, and usage information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to: provide, maintain, and improve our services; process your requests and transactions; send you technical notices and support messages; communicate with you about products, services, and events; and protect against fraud and abuse.

3. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with service providers who perform services on our behalf, in response to legal requests, or to protect our rights.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to: access your personal information; correct inaccurate data; delete your information; restrict or object to processing; and data portability. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@luxscale.ai.

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to collect information about your browsing activities. You can control cookies through your browser settings, but disabling cookies may limit your use of certain features.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that we have collected information from a child, we will delete it immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@luxscale.ai
