About LuxSCale AI

The first AI specialized in lighting design and analysis

Our Story

LuxSCale AI was born from a vision to revolutionize the lighting design industry. We recognized that lighting professionals needed a powerful, intelligent tool that could analyze, recommend, and optimize lighting solutions with unprecedented accuracy.

Built by a team of lighting engineers, AI specialists, and designers, LuxSCale AI combines decades of lighting expertise with cutting-edge artificial intelligence to deliver results that were previously impossible.

Today, LuxSCale AI serves lighting designers, architects, and engineers worldwide, helping them create better-lit spaces more efficiently than ever before.

Our Mission

To democratize expert-level lighting design by making advanced AI-powered tools accessible to every lighting professional, regardless of their technical background or project scale.

Our Vision

A world where every space is optimally lit, energy-efficient, and designed with the well-being of its occupants in mind. We envision LuxSCale AI as the global standard for intelligent lighting design.
