What we're doing: draw.html is a standalone room-drawing tool (unfolded projection, wall colors, dimensions). LuxScaleAI has a full lighting calc backend (Flask + IES). The goal is to make the room drawing tool part of the study flow — pulling real room dimensions from the calculator, overlaying fixture positions, and linking to the result page.
